What this tool does automatically

Pick prospects, hit Start outreach, and each one is queued a three-message sequence:

Day 0
Intro email — the offer, personalised to that business
Day 4
Short nudge
Day 11
Final note, then it stops

Every message is written and scheduled up front, so you can read exactly what will go out before anything sends. Logging a reply cancels the rest of that sequence immediately.

The guardrails

Cold B2B email is legal in the US under CAN-SPAM when it's done properly, so the rules are built in rather than left to you:

  • Sending is blocked until your business name, address and a real mailing address are filled in.
  • Every email carries your postal address and a plain-English opt-out. It can't be edited out.
  • "STOP" or anything like it suppresses that address and its whole domain, permanently.
  • A daily cap (default 40) stops you burning your sending domain.
  • Mail goes through your mail account, so replies come to you.

Texting isn't automated here on purpose — cold automated SMS falls under the TCPA, which needs prior consent. Use the call and walk-in scripts instead.
